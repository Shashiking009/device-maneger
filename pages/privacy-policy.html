Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

At Calculator Hub (accessible from https://calculator-hub.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Calculator Hub and how we use it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Calculator Hub is designed with privacy-first client-side architecture. All calculation inputs (such as numbers, grades, financial figures, medical dimensions, or passwords) are processed entirely inside your local web browser using JavaScript. We do not collect, transmit, or store your private calculation data on any external servers.

2. LocalStorage & Cookies

Calculator Hub uses browser LocalStorage to remember your user interface preferences (such as Light or Dark mode visual theme) and your list of recently used calculators. You can clear your LocalStorage cache at any time through your browser settings.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense

Google is one of our third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to calculator-hub.com and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Log Files

Calculator Hub follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

5. CCPA & GDPR Compliance

Under CCPA and GDPR regulations, users have the right to request access, rectification, or deletion of any personal data. Because Calculator Hub does not collect personal identity information, no personal records are retained.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
