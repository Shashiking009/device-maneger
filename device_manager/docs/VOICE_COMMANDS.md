# 🎙️ SPIDY AI — VOICE COMMAND REFERENCE

## Activation Phrase
Say **"Hey Spidy"** (or variations *"Hey Spidey"*, *"Hey Spider"*, *"A Spider"*).

## Supported Voice Commands

### Special Windows Folders
- *"Hey Spidy, open Downloads"*
- *"Hey Spidy, open my Desktop folder"*
- *"Hey Spidy, take me to Documents"*
- *"Hey Spidy, open Pictures"*
- *"Hey Spidy, open that folder"* (Resolves from previous turn context)

### Applications & Multi-Action
- *"Hey Spidy, open Chrome"*
- *"Hey Spidy, launch Visual Studio Code"*
- *"Hey Spidy, open Chrome and Notepad"*
- *"Hey Spidy, close it"* (Resolves from previous turn context)

### System & Laptop Status
- *"Hey Spidy, how is my laptop?"*
- *"Hey Spidy, what application am I using?"*
- *"Hey Spidy, increase volume"* / *"Make it louder"*
- *"Hey Spidy, lock laptop"*

### AI & Knowledge Queries
- *"Hey Spidy, what is Python?"*
- *"Hey Spidy, who created it?"* (Resolves `"it"` to Python)
- *"Hey Spidy, explain quantum computing"*
